FAQ

Frequently asked

Do I need an account to use Tallero?
No account, ever. There's no sign-up and no login. You open the app and start scoring — everything lives right on your iPhone.
How much does Tallero cost?
Tallero is free during beta — every game included, no ads. We're still finalizing how pricing will work after launch, and we'll announce it here before anything changes.
Which games are built in?
Twenty games to start: Hearts, Cribbage, Yahtzee, Euchre, Rummy, Gin Rummy, Spades, Phase 10, Farkle, Flip 7, Dutch Blitz, Dominoes, Cornhole, Golf, Mini Golf, Mölkky, Qwirkle, and more — plus Custom Score for anything not on the list, and Quick Score and Quick Timer for instant setups.
Can I score a game that isn't listed?
Absolutely. Use Custom Score to set up players, a target score, and whether high or low wins. Tallero keeps the running totals for any game you can imagine.
Does Tallero work offline?
Completely. Tallero never needs a connection. All of your games, rosters, and preferences are stored on your device, so it works on a plane, a cabin, or anywhere the Wi-Fi doesn't reach.
I made a mistake entering a score — can I fix it?
Yes. Every game has Undo and Redo pinned to the action bar, so a misclick is never a problem. Step back as many entries as you need.
How do I reset or clear my data?
Open Settings → Reset all data. This wipes every saved game, roster, and preference from your device. It can't be undone, so use it with care.
I found a bug, or have an idea. What now?
We'd love to hear it. Email support@gettallero.com with what happened (and your iPhone model and iOS version, if it's a bug). Feature and game requests genuinely shape what comes next.
