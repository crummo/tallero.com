Why Tallero

It knows the games.

Not a blank grid with a calculator attached. Tallero knows a Hearts hand adds to 26, what a Farkle bust is, and that Gin scores two different ways — so you tap what happened, not what it's worth.

Every game, properly

Hearts checks the hand adds up and spots a shot moon. Phase 10 tracks phases, not just points. Farkle knows a bust. Each game brings its own rules, not a generic tally.

Score anything

Custom Score for any game on Earth. Set targets, rounds, and high- or low-wins — Tallero handles the totals.

The rules, in your pocket

Every game ships with its own rules sheet — how to play, how it scores, and the variants your table argues about. No phone-hunting mid-hand.

Quick Score & Timer

One tap from the home screen to a blank scoreboard or a countdown. Perfect for a game you're inventing as you go.

Rosters that remember

Save your regular players with their own colors. Drop them into any game in a tap — no retyping names.

An end to every night

The final screen isn't just a total. Awards for the run nobody saw coming, the comeback, the collapse — and a line for every player at the table, not only the winner.

Light & dark mode

A clean, readable board in any lighting — bright kitchen table or dim back porch. It follows your phone, or pick one.

Records that stick around

Every night is remembered: leaderboards, win rates, house records, and who your archrival is. Break the best-ever round and Tallero says so — and tells you whose record you just took.

The lineup

Seventeen games,
one app.

The classics and the game-night staples, each scored by its own rules — plus Custom Score, Quick Score and a Timer.

Built for the table

Pick up right where you left off.

Tallero keeps every game in progress so a paused round is never a lost round. Come back tomorrow and your scores are exactly where you left them.

  • Resume any game from the home screen
  • Undo & redo any entry — fix a misclick, no drama
  • A leader crown that makes the standings obvious
  • Easy on the eyes in full dark mode
