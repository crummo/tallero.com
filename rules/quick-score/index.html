Quick Score: a score counter for any game

Tap to count, hold to subtract, set a target if you're racing to one. For the games Tallero doesn't have built in — or the ones you're making up as you go.

Quick Score is the fastest way to keep score in Tallero: one tap from the home screen, no setup, no rules. Each player gets a tile, and every tap adds a point.

How it works

Do thisResult
Tap a player's tile+1
Tap +5 or +10+5 / +10
Press and hold the tile, +5 or +10subtract
Swipe a tile down−1
  • + Player adds as many players as you need, and tapping a name renames it.
  • Racing to a number? Set a target and the first player past it wins automatically.
  • Tapped the wrong player? Hold their tile to take the point back off.

When to use it

  • A game Tallero doesn't have a built-in scorer for.
  • A game you're inventing as you go.
  • Anything where you just need a tally: points in a backyard game, rounds won, who's buying the next round.

Want the same setup every week, with a target and high- or low-wins? Use Custom instead and save it as its own tile.
