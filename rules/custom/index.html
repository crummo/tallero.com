Custom: score any game your way

Set up players, a target and how the game is won — then save it as its own tile for next time.

Custom is for any game Tallero doesn't have built in. You choose how it's scored and how it's won, and Tallero keeps the totals, crowns the winner and keeps the stats — just like a built-in game.

How the game is won

RaceFirst to the target wins.
ExactHit the target exactly; go over and you bust back — to half, to zero, or the throw simply doesn't count.
AvoidFirst to the target is out, and the lowest score wins.

With no target, the game runs for your set number of rounds, and you choose whether the highest or lowest total wins.

Save it for next time

Save a Custom setup and it becomes its own tile on the home screen, so your house-rules game is one tap away next week.

Custom or Quick Score?

Quick Score is a blank tap counter for when you just need a tally. Custom is for when the game has a target, a way to win, or rounds you want tracked — and when you want it in your stats.
