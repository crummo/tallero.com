Quick Timer: a countdown timer for game night

Turn limits, drawing rounds, sand-timer games — a big, simple countdown that's one tap from the home screen.

Quick Timer is a countdown timer built for the table: big ring, big buttons, and a chime when time's up.

How it works

  • Pick a time: tap a preset — 30, 60, 90 or 120 seconds — or type any number of seconds.
  • Or drag the ring to set the time directly.
  • Start it with the button, or tap the ring to start and pause.
  • Need more time? Add 30 seconds or a minute while it's running.
  • When it runs out you'll hear a chime and see Time's up! — then Reset to go again.
  • It keeps accurate time even if your phone locks mid-countdown.

Good for

  • Turn limits for players who take too long.
  • Drawing and guessing games like Pictionary or charades.
  • Any game that came with a sand timer you can't find.

Always one tap away.

Quick Timer sits next to Quick Score on Tallero's home screen.
