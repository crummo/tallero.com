Privacy Policy

The short version: Tallero doesn't collect your data. It stays on your device, with you.

Tallero (the "App") is made by Slow Dust Software ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Contact: support@gettallero.com

The short version

Tallero is built to be private by default:

  • Your game data stays on your device. Players, scores, rosters, and settings are stored locally — we don't have a server that collects them, and there's no account to create.
  • No ads. No selling your data. Ever.
  • The only things that leave your device are anonymous usage statistics (which you can turn off) and — if you make a purchase — the information needed to deliver what you bought.
  • Board photos never leave your device. The camera is used only to count the dots on a finished Tacta board, on your phone, and the photo is discarded straight afterwards.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

The rest of this document spells that out.

Information stored on your device (not collected by us)

Everything you create in the App — player names, scores, game history, saved rosters, and your preferences — is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to us and is not backed up to any server we control. If you delete the App, that data is removed with it. You can also clear it from within the App's settings.

Because there are no accounts, we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, or your location.

The camera

Tacta's board scanner can photograph a finished board and count the dots for you, so you don't have to tally them by hand. When you use it, iOS asks for camera permission.

The photo never leaves your device. It is decoded into pixels in the App, the dots are counted, and the image is discarded. It is not uploaded, not saved to your photo library, not attached to analytics or diagnostics, and never seen by us or by anyone else. There is no server involved at any point — the counting happens entirely on your phone.

You can decline camera access and keep using Tacta normally; the scanner is an optional shortcut, and every score can be entered by tapping the number pad.

Information that does leave your device

Anonymous usage analytics

To understand which games and features get used, the App may send anonymous, aggregated usage events (for example, "a game was opened" or "a game finished," along with the game type, the App version, and which build it came from). These events contain no personal information, and we do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

You can turn this off at any time in the App's settings; when off, no usage events are sent.

Crash and error diagnostics — none

The App sends no crash or error reports. When something goes wrong it recovers on its own screen and offers you a way to email us about it — nothing is transmitted unless you choose to write.

If we ever add automatic diagnostics to help fix a bug, they will be configured not to include personal information (such as your IP address), they will exist only to keep the App stable, and we will update this policy and its effective date before that happens.

Purchases (tips and any optional upgrades)

If you choose to make a purchase, payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment; we never receive or store your credit card or payment details.

To deliver what you bought — and to restore it if you reinstall or change devices — we use a purchase-management service (RevenueCat). It receives the fact of your purchase and which items you're entitled to, along with the purchase identifier described below. It does not receive your payment details.

Your purchase identifier

So that purchases you've already paid for aren't lost, the App creates a random identifier (a UUID) the first time it runs and stores it in the iOS Keychain. This identifier:

  • is random — it is not derived from your name, email, device serial, or any advertising identifier;
  • is specific to Tallero and is not used to track you across other apps or websites;
  • persists if you delete and reinstall the App, and — because the Keychain syncs through iCloud — may follow you to another of your devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

That durability is deliberate and exists for one reason: tips are consumable purchases with no Apple-side "Restore Purchases" path, so without a stable identifier a reinstall would silently orphan what you paid for.

We are describing this plainly because it is the one identifier the App keeps that persists across reinstalls. It is shared only with RevenueCat, only to match you to your own purchases.

Service providers we rely on

We use a small number of third parties to provide the functions above. Each receives only what it needs for that function:

  • Apple — App Store distribution and in-app purchases. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — purchase and entitlement management (which items you own, and restoring them). Receives your purchase identifier and purchase/entitlement records; does not receive payment details. See their privacy policy.
  • PostHog — privacy-focused, anonymous usage analytics, hosted in the European Union. Covered by the analytics opt-out in the App's settings. See their privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone, and we do not allow these providers to use your information for their own purposes.

How we use information

Only to operate and improve the App: keeping your scores on your device, counting the dots on a board you photograph, understanding which features are used (in aggregate), and completing and restoring purchases you initiate. That's it.

Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data persists until you delete it or uninstall the App. You're in control.
  • Anonymous analytics are retained only as long as needed to analyze usage, then aged out. The same would apply to diagnostics if they are ever added.
  • Purchase records (and the identifier they're keyed to) are retained while needed to keep your purchases restorable. You can ask us to delete them — see below — though doing so means purchases can no longer be restored to you.

Children's privacy

Tallero is a general-audience scorekeeper and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Analytics opt-out — toggle it off in the App's settings; no usage events are sent thereafter.
  • Your local data — view, change, or delete it any time within the App, or by uninstalling.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Almost everything the App holds is stored on your device and is yours to delete directly. The one exception is the purchase identifier and purchase records described above — contact us at the address below and we'll retrieve or delete them for you.

International users

Usage analytics are processed in the European Union (we use PostHog's EU cloud). Purchase records may be processed by our other service providers in the United States or elsewhere.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we'll update this policy and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact
