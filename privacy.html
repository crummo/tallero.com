Privacy Policy

The short version: Tallero doesn't collect your data. It stays on your device, with you.

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Overview

Tallero ("the app", "we", "us") is a score-keeping app for card games and game nights. We built Tallero to be private by design. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking.

This policy explains what that means in practice and what limited information is involved when you use Tallero.

Information we collect

None. Tallero does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, or any other personal information. We have no servers that receive your data, and there is no analytics or advertising SDK collecting information in the background.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create in Tallero — your games in progress, saved player rosters, names, scores, and app preferences — is stored locally on your device only. We never see it, and it is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

This data is included in your device's normal encrypted backups (for example, an iCloud or finder/iTunes backup of your iPhone) if you have those enabled. Those backups are managed entirely by Apple under Apple's terms, not by us.

Payments & the App Store

Tallero is free during beta. If we introduce a paid version later, any purchase would be processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, payment card, or billing details — Apple handles the entire transaction and shares only anonymized, aggregate sales information with us. Apple's handling of that purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

Tallero includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software of its own, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Apple provides developers with anonymous, aggregate usage and crash statistics through the App Store's built-in analytics — and only for people who have opted in at the system level (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on your device). That information comes from Apple, never identifies you, and reaches us only as summary numbers. You can turn it off at any time in those iOS settings.

If we ever add our own usage analytics, we'll update this page and the app's App Store privacy details beforehand — see "Changes to this policy" below.

Children's privacy

Tallero is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing for us to access, retain, or delete.

Deleting your data

You are always in control of your information:

  • To clear everything within the app, go to Settings → Reset all data. This permanently removes all saved games, rosters, and preferences from your device.
  • To remove the app and its data entirely, delete Tallero from your device the way you would any other app.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Tallero handles data — for example, by adding optional cloud sync or crash reporting in a future update — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be made clear before they take effect.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email us at support@gettallero.com and a real person will get back to you.
